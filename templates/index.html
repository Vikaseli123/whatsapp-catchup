How do I export a chat?

In WhatsApp, open the chat details, choose Export chat, then choose Without media. Upload the exported .txt or .zip file here.

For group chats, add names separated by commas or new lines. This lets @all and @everyone correctly include you. Leave blank for direct chats; sender names are read from the export.
